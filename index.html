Turtles are amazing animals. They have a hard shell on their back that protects them from danger. When a turtle is scared, it can hide its head, legs, and tail inside the shell.

There are different kinds of turtles. Some live in water, like sea turtles. Others live on land, and we call them tortoises. Water turtles have webbed feet to help them swim, while land turtles have strong, stumpy legs for walking.
